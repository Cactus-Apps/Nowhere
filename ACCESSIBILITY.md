# Accessibility Statement

Nowhere is committed to making our app accessible to everyone, regardless of ability or technology. We believe in inclusive navigation and continuously work to improve the accessibility of our apps for people with disabilities.

## Our accessibility statement

This accessibility statement applies to the Nowhere app. We aim to make Nowhere as accessible as we can, recognising that accessible technology supports all of us. Our goal is to enable everyone to navigate their world freely.

## Accessibility standards

We strive to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, wherever reasonably practicable. These guidelines help us make digital content more accessible for people with disabilities and more usable for everyone.

## What we are doing

We are continuously reviewing our app design, navigation flows, colour contrast, font sizes and touch targets to improve accessibility. This includes support for screen readers, adjustable text sizes and sufficient colour contrast for people with low vision.

## Conformance status

Nowhere is an open-source project built on community data. While we work towards full WCAG 2.1 AA conformance, some areas may not yet be fully accessible due to the nature of mobile maps and user-generated data. We are committed to improving these areas over time.

## Reporting accessibility problems

If you find any part of the Nowhere app difficult to use or access, or if you have ideas on how we could improve, please contact us. We take accessibility feedback seriously and will review every report.

## Feedback & contact

You can reach us via GitHub (Cactus-Apps/Nowhere), our website nowhereapp.vercel.app, or email the address listed in the app. We normally respond to accessibility feedback within a few days.
